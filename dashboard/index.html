How the implied value is built

Every row is scored by the same formula, from the snapshot's own figures only. No protocol name is ever special-cased.

implied = TVL × 0.205 × growthMult × maturityMult
growthMult   = 1 + clamp(change_7d, −60, +120) / 100 × 0.5
maturityMult = 0.6 + min(age_days, 1095) / 1095 × 0.4   (age unknown: 365 days)

Where 0.205 comes from

0.205 is the median mcap/TVL of the 128 live protocols in this same snapshot that report a market cap (15 protocols whose market cap reads 0 are excluded). It is a calibration from the snapshot itself, not a round number chosen by hand. The spread is wide — the middle half of live ratios runs from 0.044 to 1.043 — so any single implied value is an estimate with a wide band, and the board is a ranking, not a price.

What growth and maturity are

Growth (7-day TVL change) and maturity (days listed without a token) are directional priors, not fitted results: on the live protocols neither predicts the market-cap multiple (correlation −0.149 for growth, −0.004 for age). A growing, long-running protocol is worth more than an identical shrinking, young one — that is the task's own rule — but the weights cannot be calibrated from this data, so they are kept small and stated here.

A size damp was tested and removed

An earlier version multiplied by (TVL / $154M)−0.25, because across all live protocols market cap grows roughly with the square root of TVL (log-log slope 0.52). A blind holdout killed it: the damp made the median prediction error worse than a flat multiple in both hash splits (88.7% vs 87.9%, 90.0% vs 88.6%), the size effect reverses inside Dexs (slope 1.78) and Lending (1.50) — the global slope is a mix-of-categories artefact — and the ranking with the damp is identical to the ranking without it (Spearman 1.000, 0 of 183 rows move more than 10 ranks). The shipped model is linear in TVL.

Limits, stated plainly

  • Implied value is a circulating-style estimate, not FDV. The snapshot carries circulating market cap only, so the 0.205 multiple is a mcap/TVL multiple. The implied value estimates what a launch would be worth on the same basis — it is not a fully-diluted valuation.
  • TVL dominates the ranking. TVL spans a hundredfold across the board while growth and maturity move a value by at most about 30%, so the order is mostly the TVL order. The model changes the dollar values and the close calls, not the shape of the list.
  • "Records no token" is a data statement, not a fact about the world. It means DefiLlama's record for the protocol carries no market cap, no CoinGecko id and no symbol. A token could exist outside that record.
  • Farm plans never claim a points program or an airdrop. The snapshot carries none, so no step promises one. Each plan is six concrete steps — wallet, position, weekly action, hold, claim check, log — with a cost and a time, derived from the protocol's category, chains, TVL and age.

How each farm plan is built

Every ranked row carries a farm plan of six concrete steps. The plan is generated from the protocol's own record only — its category, first chain, TVL and age — by the fixed rules below, never by hand. No step promises a points program or an airdrop: the snapshot carries none. Each step is capped at 600 characters.

  1. Fresh wallet. Use a fresh wallet that holds only the funds you plan to farm, and keep its seed phrase offline. Cost: under $3 of gas for the first transaction. Time: 5 minutes.
  2. Open the position. The action depends on the category (table below). Cost: the capital below, plus about $8 of gas for a $150 or $250 position, about $4 for a $50 or $100 one. Time: 15 minutes to set up.
  3. One qualifying action each week, such as a deposit, a borrow, a swap or a vote. Cost: about $2 of gas a week. Time: 10 minutes a week.
  4. Hold the position for at least 60 days if the protocol is under 180 days old, 45 days if under a year, otherwise 30 days (30 days when the listing date is unknown), so the activity is counted in more than one snapshot. Cost: the capital stays in the position. Time: the hold itself.
  5. Claim check. Before you withdraw, open the protocol's own points or rewards page and claim only from the link it publishes. Cost: no fee to check, and never pay to claim. Time: 10 minutes.
  6. Farm log. Write the wallet, the dates and every transaction in a farm log so you can repeat the position or unwind it. Cost: nothing but the log. Time: 5 minutes.

Capital by TVL

$250 at TVL $5B or more; $150 at $1B or more; $100 at $100M or more; $50 below that.
